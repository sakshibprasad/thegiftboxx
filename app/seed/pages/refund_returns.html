1. No-Return / No-Refund Nature of Our Products

The Gift Boxx specializes in curated wooden gift boxes containing premium items such as watches, headphones, candles, and accessories. These products are gift-oriented, carefully assembled, and often customized or curated for specific occasions.

Accordingly, we do not accept returns or offer refunds in the following cases:

  • Change of mind after placing an order
  • Incorrect product selection, box type, variant, or quantity chosen by the customer
  • Incorrect or incomplete delivery address provided by the customer
  • Minor variations in:
    • Wood grain, colour, knots, texture, or polish
    • Packaging materials, ribbons, fillers, or tags
    • Product shades due to lighting or screen differences
  • Personalized or customized orders, including:
    • Engraving, printed names, dates, messages, or logos
    • Custom gift notes or special instructions
  • Discounted, sale, or promotional items

These scenarios do not qualify for return, replacement, or refund.

2. Eligible Cases for Replacement or Refund

A replacement or refund may be considered only in the following verified situations:

All such requests are subject to strict verification and approval by The Gift Boxx.
